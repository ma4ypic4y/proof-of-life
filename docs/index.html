Proof of Life.

In a soup of 131,072 random programs, two programs that cannot copy themselves collide, with no mutation. One of the pieces that comes out copies itself, backwards, into every one of the 25664 possible partners. We reconstructed that collision byte for byte and proved it in Lean.
